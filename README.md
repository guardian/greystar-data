# Greystar national apartment unit data

This data has a breakdown of apartment units overseen by Greystar – a private real estate company that is the  largest owner and manager of apartments in the US.

The data has the number of apartment units by city and by state as of December 2025. The more than 1 million US apartments Greystar managed as of December included 143,504 it owned and another 862,910 owned by other landlords.

The breakdown is based on an analysis by [Private Equity Stakeholder Project](https://pestakeholder.org/about-us/our-mission-and-values/), a nonprofit watchdog group, of data provided by [Yardi Matrix](https://www.yardimatrix.com/), a leading commercial real estate intelligence firm.
