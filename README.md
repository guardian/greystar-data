# Greystar national apartment unit data

This data set shows a state-by-state and local markets breakdown of apartment units overseen as of December 2025 by Greystar – a private real estate company that is the  largest owner and manager of apartments in the US. The more than 1 million US apartments Greystar managed as of December included 143,504 it owned and another 862,910 owned by other landlords.

The breakdown is based on an analysis by Private Equity Stakeholder Project, a nonprofit watchdog group, of data provided by Yardi Matrix, a leading commercial real estate intelligence firm.
